/02 — METHOD

Four parts of a better trading process.

Use a clear workflow instead of chasing every move in the market.

01

Learn

Build a strong foundation in market structure, technical concepts and trading terminology.

02

Analyze

Read context, identify levels and define the idea before thinking about execution.

03

Manage risk

Know where the idea is invalidated and size positions around acceptable risk.

04

Execute

Follow the plan, document decisions and improve through consistency rather than emotion.

05

Review

Use your results and mistakes as data to refine the process over time.

06

Automate

Where appropriate, use tools and automation to reduce repetitive work without removing risk.
